PATENTED TECHNOLOGY

Electric Propulsion Engine

WO2024121600A1

Electric propulsion engine by electromagnetic waves

This patent publication describes an electric propulsion concept based on electromagnetic fields and forces for potential aerospace and spacecraft applications.

According to the patent specification, the proposed system incorporates electromagnetic components including coils, magnets, electrically charged elements, and electronic circuitry.

The patent describes two principal approaches for producing the proposed propulsion effect: one involving a continuously rotating magnetic field in relation to an electric current, and another involving a linear magnetic field combined with a rectified electrical waveform.

Performance characteristics and potential applications described on this page are presented as information from the published patent record and should not be interpreted as independent validation of the underlying technical claims.

Electromagnetic Propulsion

The patent describes a propulsion concept based on electromagnetic fields, electrically charged components, coils, magnets, and electronic circuitry.
