AIMS & SCOPE

Research Across Scientific Boundaries

XRI Journal provides a scholarly platform for interdisciplinary research that connects theories, methods, experiments, and technological applications across multiple scientific domains.

The journal welcomes original investigations that address complex questions through the integration of knowledge from more than one discipline. Particular emphasis is placed on methodological rigor, transparent scientific reasoning, reproducibility, and responsible interpretation of evidence.

Fundamental & Applied Sciences

Energy & Sustainability

Energy systems, sustainable technologies, materials, built environments, and environmental research.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

Double-Anonymous Peer Review

Research manuscripts submitted to XRI Journal are evaluated through a double-anonymous peer-review process. During peer review, the identities of authors and reviewers are not disclosed to one another.

Editorial assessment is based on scientific relevance, methodological quality, clarity of presentation, originality, and the strength of the evidence supporting the conclusions.

Detailed peer-review procedures, editorial responsibilities, publication ethics, and author requirements will be provided through the journal's dedicated policy pages.
