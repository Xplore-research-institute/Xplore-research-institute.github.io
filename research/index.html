WHAT WE INVESTIGATE

Research Areas

Interdisciplinary research connecting fundamental questions with measurable evidence and practical innovation.

01

Gravity & Fundamental Physics

Research on motion, fields, vacuum dynamics, gravitation, and alternative theoretical descriptions of physical systems.

  • Theoretical models
  • Solar-system dynamics
  • Foundations of mechanics
02

Experimental Measurement

Design and analysis of experiments involving motion, magnetic assemblies, precision sensing, and data acquisition.

  • Instrumentation
  • Signal analysis
  • Reproducible protocols
03

Materials & Construction

Sustainable composite materials, low-carbon construction systems, structural performance, and resilient design.

  • Earth-based composites
  • Mechanical testing
  • Building systems
04

Energy & Applied Technology

Exploratory work in energy systems, scientific tools, sensing platforms, and technology transfer.

  • Energy systems
  • Prototype development
  • Open scientific tools
