COLLABORATION PROFILE

Aerospace Propulsion Research

ArianXplore is an aerospace technology organization focused on electric propulsion concepts and advanced propulsion systems for space applications.

The collaboration between Xplore Research Institute and ArianXplore focuses on research and development related to satellite thruster technologies and interdisciplinary investigations in aerospace propulsion.

This collaboration supports the exchange of scientific and technical knowledge in areas involving propulsion systems, electromagnetic technologies, experimental research, and future space applications.

TECHNOLOGY FOCUS

Electric Propulsion for Space Applications

ArianXplore's technology focus includes electric propulsion concepts for spacecraft and research into propulsion approaches involving electromagnetic phenomena.

Its official website presents electrothermal, electrostatic, and electromagnetic propulsion concepts and describes its work on an electromagnetic-wave propulsion system intended for aerospace applications.
