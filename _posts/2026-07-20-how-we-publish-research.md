A strong research publication should clearly distinguish observations, calculations, interpretations, and unresolved questions.

## Publication structure

Each scientific article should ideally include:

1. A precise research question
2. Background and relevant literature
3. Methods and materials
4. Results
5. Interpretation
6. Limitations
7. Data and supplementary materials
8. References

## Versioning

GitHub preserves the history of every change. This makes it possible to correct errors, update datasets, and show how a research document has developed over time.
